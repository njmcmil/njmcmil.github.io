In Quarry, an enemy can use what it sees and hears to infer where a player is. I built the enemy logic with Unreal Engine's StateTree and AI Perception systems. Sight and sound supplied the cues, and the StateTree organized how the enemy responded to them.

My multiplayer work included the inventory system and replication of gameplay and animations across players' clients. I also used procedural content generation to create caves and made models in Blender for the game.
