I'm Nathan McMillan, a computer science senior at Clemson University with a minor in artificial intelligence. I expect to graduate in December 2026.

At Clemson's AutoAI Research Laboratory, I work on visual inspection of engine bays. I built a prototype that lets a language-model agent use an object detector, and I trained the detector locally so the inspection images could stay on the machine. [Read about the inspection prototype](/posts/portfolio-website-development/).

Before joining the lab, I interned with Clemson Computing & Information Technology. I installed classroom equipment and worked with the network and systems teams to trace audiovisual and connectivity problems.

My [projects](/projects/) also explore how robots learn from demonstrations, how vision-language models identify wildfire smoke, and how game characters respond to what they see and hear.
